## Implemented domain rules

- Tenant-owned catalogue, customer, order, quote, payment, and stock operations scope records to the active tenant and enforce server-side permissions.
- Stock is reserved atomically for tracked storefront products and released transactionally when an unpaid reservation expires. The worker has a persistent delayed job plus a database sweep for overdue holds.
- Storefront checkout accepts current server-side product prices; clients cannot submit prices or totals. Currency and tax are calculated in integer minor units. Delivery tax is included in tax totals while the displayed delivery fee is the configured gross amount.
- A storefront order records immutable customer, address, policy, and payment-instruction snapshots. It remains `pending_payment`; public checkout cannot set it paid. A staff ledger capture is required to record funds received.
- Manual captures and refunds are idempotent, audited and bounded against the order balance. Payment, order and fulfilment states are independent.
- Quote revisions are immutable, acceptance uses a private expiring link, and conversion uses the accepted revision without silent repricing.
- Appointment slots are derived from tenant-local business hours and service/resource buffers using real instants: nonexistent spring-forward times are omitted and both fall-back offsets remain selectable. All-day closures use actual timezone date bounds (including 23/25-hour DST days). Booking and rescheduling recheck capacity inside replica-set transactions while locking the relevant resource records; holds expire after ten minutes, and status transitions are append-only evented and audited.
- Confirmed appointments can receive a 256-bit private customer link that expires after the appointment and is stored only as a token hash. Reissuing replaces the prior token. Customers can cancel or reschedule before the configured cutoff; public slot selection rechecks service, business hours, closures and resource capacity in a transaction. Customer-originated changes are audited, and terminal staff actions revoke links. Appointment notification templates are not connected yet, so staff copy and share those links manually.
- Team invitations use random single-use tokens stored only as hashes, expire after 48 hours, and are accepted transactionally. Existing accounts must prove their password; new accounts receive the invitee-selected password. Role changes and revocations are audited, revocation invalidates workspace sessions, and a workspace cannot lose its last active owner. If a workspace has no verified SMTP connection, an owner privately shares the returned invite URL; otherwise the invite is sent through the encrypted outbox, with delivery state visible in Email delivery settings.
- Deal stages are configurable per workspace; New stays first, Won and Lost stay final, and stages with live deals cannot be removed. Sales members see and change only deals assigned to themselves; owners can reassign active team members. Lost reasons and every creation, edit, assignment and stage change are recorded in tenant-scoped history/audit records. Won is only reachable from the last active stage; reopening terminal outcomes requires an owner.
- Support cases, case events and return authorizations are tenant-scoped. Returns are requested against a physical line of a fulfilled order; pending and authorized requests reserve the unreturned quantity, and rejected requests release it. Authorization, decline and received quantities are separately recorded. A received tracked item updates stock only when staff explicitly record receipt; this does not issue a payment refund.
- Store receipt bearer tokens are hashed for lookup and encrypted with AES-256-GCM at rest for idempotent link recovery. The key must be separately configured and backed up.
- JSON write APIs use a streaming byte limit even when `Content-Length` is absent. Public checkout also enforces same-origin requests, schema validation, tenant-specific rate limiting, policy-version confirmation, and a unique idempotency key. Storefront dialogs support keyboard focus trapping, focus restoration, and Escape; the checkout error is announced to assistive technology.
- Demo customers and transactions are not silently created during initial setup.

## Current validation

Current verification on 2026-10-06: 70 unit tests pass and one opt-in MongoDB test skips by default; the optimized Next.js build and TypeScript check pass. Catalogue CSV setup/import/export, invalid-batch atomicity, duplicate rejection, authentication, same-origin enforcement, work-order attachments/delete cleanup, and packing slips passed against disposable replica-set API runs. MFA enrollment/recovery, physical dispatch, and mixed physical/service order completion, idempotency, and payment separation passed against isolated MongoDB replica sets. MFA login challenge with Redis, inbox database/browser acceptance, and complete release readiness remain unverified. These checks do not establish full release readiness.

Not yet verified: clean installation on a buyer environment; all spec E2E cases, including dashboard total reconciliation; tenant-isolation and role-attack matrices; concurrent checkout under live replica-set contention; payment provider events; worker restart and backup restore; accessibility and browser/device acceptance; production deployment; and CodeCanyon packaging, license, and buyer documentation.

## Remaining first-release work

Customer portal, shared web inbox, and catalogue CSV still need comprehensive tenant-isolation/security and browser acceptance evidence; quote approval routes need replica-set workflow acceptance. Private work-order attachments, deletion, failed-delete retry, and age-based orphan cleanup pass isolated production API/worker acceptance; browser acceptance, malware scanning, active-file retention, and backup/restore remain. Password reset, quote/order/appointment email notifications and templates; return shipping labels; live Stripe account/webhook acceptance; WooCommerce and WhatsApp/OpenAI integrations; outbound APIs/webhooks; scoped AI and durable automation beyond hold expiry; agency/platform plans and billing; analytics, diagnostics, broader backup/restore and update/rollback; comprehensive E2E/isolation/recovery evidence; and the complete CodeCanyon buyer package all remain in scope. MFA settings have been exercised against a replica set, but sign-in challenge acceptance remains unverified. Portal and appointment links are still manually shared. Staff appointment creation, week calendar, weekday-hour editing, timezone-aware date closures and customer cancellation/rescheduling are available.
